Privacy

Walnut: Voice Notes · Last updated October 2026

The short version

Walnut collects nothing. Your recordings, transcripts, notes, dictations and settings are created and kept on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else, and there's no account.

What stays on your iPhone

Audio you record, the text Walnut transcribes, notes it writes, your dictation history, your dictionary and snippets. Speech recognition, speaker labels and writing all run on the device using Apple's on-device models and the models you choose to download. This data is left out of iCloud Backup.

When Walnut uses the internet

Only to download models when you ask: Kiwi, Mango and the speaker-label model come from Hugging Face, and Apple may download speech models. These downloads send no recordings or text. Purchases go through Apple and the App Store.

Keyboard and Full Access

The Walnut keyboard types without Full Access. Voice typing needs Full Access only so the keyboard can hand your words between the Walnut app and the app you're typing in, on this iPhone. The keyboard has no network code and doesn't record what you type.

Calendar and notifications

If you allow it, Walnut reads your calendar on the device to name notes after meetings and to remind you before they start. It never changes your calendar.

Recording other people

You're responsible for getting consent before recording others. In many places the law requires it.

Deleting your data

Delete a note from its menu, or everything from Settings › Delete all notes and audio. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

Walnut isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on our website before it takes effect.
