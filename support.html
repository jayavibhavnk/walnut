Walnut: Voice Notes for iPhone

Does anything leave my iPhone?

No. Recordings, transcripts, notes and dictations stay on the device. Walnut uses the internet only to download models when you tap Download, and for App Store purchases.

Why does the keyboard ask for Full Access?

Typing works without it. Voice typing needs it so the keyboard can pass your words between the Walnut app and the app you're typing in, on your iPhone. The keyboard has no network code.

Voice typing says “Turn voice on in Walnut”.

Open Walnut › Voice › Turn voice on, pick how long, then go back to your app and hold the key.

Notes aren't being written.

Fig needs Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri). Or download Kiwi or Mango in Walnut › Settings › Models; they work without Apple Intelligence, while Walnut is open.

How do I set up the Action Button?

iPhone Settings › Action Button › Controls, search for Walnut and pick “Walnut note”. Press once to start, again to stop.

How do I restore my purchase?

Walnut › Settings › About › Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

What does it cost?

14 days free, then a one-time purchase: Walnut (writes with Fig) or Walnut Pro (adds Kiwi and Mango). Every update and model update is included. No subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a note from its ⋯ menu, or everything from Settings › Delete all notes and audio. Deleting the app removes everything.
